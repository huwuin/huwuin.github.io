Example of Assumptions related to C1:
● Companies financial data are available, audited and reported in a comparable 
format.
● Historical default records exist and are labelled reliably 
● Analysts and business teams will trust and use model-based ratings if clear 
explanations are provided.
Example of Limitations related to C1:
● The study will focus only on corporate clients (SMEs and mid-sized 
companies).
● Data will be limited to companies from a small set of sectors and countries. 
● The system will support analysts’ decisions but will not automatically approve or 
reject credit applications.
Example of Challenges related to C1:
● Dealing with class imbalance, since defaults are rare events compared to 
non-defaulting companies.
● Integrating heterogeneous data sources with different update frequencies and 
formats.
● Keeping ratings accurate when economic conditions change, which requires 
monitoring model drift and recalibrating the model.
C1

Example of Assumptions related to C2:
● Public data sources provide data that is accurate and representative enough to 
measure popularity and impact.
● The selected criteria are enough to capture what makes an artist “top tier”.
● Metrics from different platforms and time periods can be normalised to a 
common scale so that they can be compared fairly.
Example of Limitations related to C2:
● The study will focus on only one domain (e.g., music artists), excluding movies 
and books.
● The proposal will present a high-level design and a theoretical approach; full 
implementation of the system is outside the scope.
● Data will mainly come from Western, English-language platforms and charts, so 
the ranking may not reflect global cultural diversity.
Example of Challenges related to C2:
● Comparing artists from different eras fairly, since older artists have little digital 
or streaming data and newer artists benefit from social media exposure.
● Turning subjective concepts such as “cultural impact” or “innovation” into 
objective, measurable indicators.
● Defining and justifying the weights of each criterion without introducing 
personal bias into the final ranking.
C2
