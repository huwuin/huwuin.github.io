Phase 3: Apply Best Practices & Avoid 
Pitfalls
13
▶ The "Golden Rule" of Simplicity:  Focus on one idea 
per chart and remove "data noise" like unnecessary 3D 
effects, shadows, or heavy gridlines.
▶ Ensure Accuracy: Start the Y-axis at zero (especially 
for bar and area charts) to avoid exaggerating small 
differences.
▶ Consistency: Use the same fonts, colours, and scales 
across similar charts to help the audience build "visual 
habits“.
▶ Audience Fit: Match the complexity of the chart to the 
reader’s expertise.

PAGE 14
Phase 4: Structure the presentation
14
▶ Introduce the graph. State the title, topic, data 
source, and time frame.
▶ Point Out Important Information: Highlight the main 
trends, patterns, or outliers using descriptive verbs 
(e.g., "rose sharply," "remained stable").
▶ Share Conclusions:  Explain what the data actually 
means for the future or what decisions should be made 
based on these insights.
