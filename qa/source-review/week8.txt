PAGE 38
The Speech Structure Building Method
(Greek Temple)
38
1 Define your foundation 
(opening) – start strong, grab 
attention, set your message.
2 Build your pillars 
(body) – choose e.g. 2 
or 3 main points 
(sub-themes) to 
support your message. 
3 Add your roof (closing)  – 
wrap up and reinforce your 
message, give a memorable 
ending. 
4 Connect roof back to 
foundation via the drainpipe  – 
link your closing back to your 
opening to give a sense of 
completeness and raise impact.

Signposting
● The use of specific phrases and words to 
guide the reader through the content of your 
talk.
● Phrases to open and close the different parts 
of a presentation.

Outlining the presentation

“Here’s an outline of what we’ll be covering today…”
“The presentation is divided into three parts… I’m going 
to cover three points/ideas today...”
“There are four main points I’d like to discuss… First of 
all, I'll... … and then I’ll go on to …” “Finally/Lastly… 
First, Then/ Secondly/Thirdly/Next ...”
“At the end of my talk/presentation, there will be a 
chance to ask questions. Please save your questions for 
the end of the talk.”
